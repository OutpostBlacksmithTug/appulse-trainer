Features

Everything in one injector loader

Six core modules that work independently or together, each with its own hotkey and config profile.

Aimbot

Configurable FOV, smoothness, and bone priority. Lock onto targets with per-game aim profiles and humanized curves.

👁️

ESP Wallhack

See player boxes, skeletons, health bars, and distance labels through walls with adjustable color and opacity.

⚡

Triggerbot

Instant-fire module with adjustable delay, hitbox filters, and hold-key activation for precise reaction timing.

🔧

No-Recoil Toggle

Neutralize vertical and horizontal recoil with a single hotkey. Tune strength per weapon class and game.

Injector Loader

Hotkey Profiles

Save and switch full config sets per game. Bind every module to its own key and load profiles instantly.

How it works

Up and running in three steps

No installers, no dependencies — just download, launch, and load your config.

1

Download the loader

Grab the latest Appulse Trainer build from the download page and extract the archive to any folder on your PC.

2

Launch & select your game

Run the injector loader as administrator. It auto-detects running processes and lets you pick the matching per-game config.

3

Inject & toggle modules

Hit inject, then use your hotkey profile to toggle aimbot, ESP, triggerbot, and no-recoil on the fly — no restart needed.
